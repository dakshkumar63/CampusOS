# Data Sources

Campus OS does not present unverified content as institutional fact. Each record must reference `sources` and identify its authority in the UI.

Initial approved public links:

- Official MSRIT website: https://www.msrit.edu/
- Official examination portal: https://exam.msrit.edu/
- MSRIT alumni: https://www.msritalumni.com/

Configure sources as `manual`, `RSS`, `API`, `crawler`, `imported document`, or `URL-only`. Before enabling a fetcher, confirm terms of service, robots rules, copyright requirements, rate limits and ownership. Community records must remain `pending_review` and carry a `Demo data` or community badge until independently verified.
